Research date: November 2, 2026. The studio sources establish release information, cast/team involvement, and film metadata. The priority tiers and weights are our recommendations, not official prerequisites or claims about the unreleased film's full plot.

## Editorial priority

High priority: Infinity War, Endgame, X-Men, Wakanda Forever, Brave New World, Thunderbolts, Fantastic Four: First Steps. Endgame's immediate setup is bundled for newcomers. Endgame receives the highest individual editorial weight (12) because the directors explicitly link its ending to the new story. Other high-priority weights range from 5 to 10. These choices favor the recent team introductions and Endgame over older optional origins. Selection maximizes weighted value within time; it does not guarantee that every high-priority film fits.

Useful context: The Avengers, Civil War, Ragnarok, Shang-Chi, Multiverse of Madness. These support team relationships or concepts without claiming all their leads are confirmed in Doomsday.

Optional origins/background: Iron Man, Thor, First Avenger, Winter Soldier, Guardians, Age of Ultron, Doctor Strange, Black Panther. They remain explorable; a short route need not include them.

The same performer playing two roles does not establish character identity. X-Men inclusion does not prove a particular timeline interpretation. Not all series/films are represented, including Loki; this is explicitly a movie-only prototype. External sources may contain spoilers.

## Time semantics

A “week” is seven calendar days of an average viewing allowance. Finish dates can split a film across sessions, and are suggestions rather than calendar bookings. Marking a film watched removes it from future time consumption and may free space for another. Doomsday has no catch-up runtime in the model. Already-passed deadlines produce an empty plan instead of negative time.
